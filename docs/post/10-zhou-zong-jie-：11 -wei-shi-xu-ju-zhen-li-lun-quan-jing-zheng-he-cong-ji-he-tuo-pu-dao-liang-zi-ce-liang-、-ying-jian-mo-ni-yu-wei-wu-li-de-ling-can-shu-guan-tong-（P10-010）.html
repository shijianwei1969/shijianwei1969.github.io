$\sum_{i=0}^{3} K_i^\dagger K_i = (1 - \delta_{\text{loop}}) I + \frac{\delta_{\text{loop}}}{3} \sum_{i=1}^3 \sigma_i^\dagger \sigma_i = (1 - \delta_{\text{loop}}) I + \delta_{\text{loop}} I = I$
