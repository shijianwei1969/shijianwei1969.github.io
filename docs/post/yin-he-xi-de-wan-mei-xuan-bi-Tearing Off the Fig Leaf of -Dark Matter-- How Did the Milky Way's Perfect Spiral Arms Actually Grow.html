Tearing Off the Fig Leaf of "Dark Matter": How Did the Milky Way's Perfect Spiral Arms Actually Grow?

作者：山东威海理论物理EPD工程组 项目总指挥 史建威
Author: Shi Jianwei, Project Director, Shandong Weihai Theoretical Physics EPD Engineering Group

当我们将詹姆斯·韦伯太空望远镜对准深空，最让人震撼的宇宙奇观，莫过于那些极其壮丽、完美对称的“旋涡星系”（比如我们的银河系）。它们就像宇宙中发光的“章鱼”，伸展着两条或四条明亮而弯曲的旋臂。

When we point the James Webb Space Telescope into deep space, the most breathtaking cosmic spectacle is undoubtedly those magnificent, perfectly symmetrical "spiral galaxies" (such as our Milky Way). They resemble luminous "octopuses" in the universe, stretching out two or four bright, curved arms.

但如果你去问今天的主流天文学家：这几条完美的旋臂是怎么形成的？为什么它们历经几十亿年都不散架？ 他们会扔给你一堆极其复杂的“密度波理论”，如果算不平，他们就会祭出那个万能的修车补丁——暗物质。

But if you ask today's mainstream astronomers: How did these perfect spiral arms form? Why have they remained intact for billions of years? They will throw at you an extremely complex set of "density wave theories," and if the calculations don't balance, they will pull out that universal patch-all — dark matter.

今天，我们要彻底撕下这块遮羞布。抛弃那些为了拼凑公式而捏造的幽灵实体，回归最纯粹的力学第一性原理。你会发现，星系旋臂的诞生，根本不需要暗物质，它只是星系根域控制的包络面扩张生长过程中，一场宏大的“内层高速天体对外层低速天体拖拽”形成的形状残留。

Today, we are going to tear off that fig leaf completely. Abandon those ghostly entities fabricated to patch up formulas, and return to the purest first principles of mechanics. You will find that the birth of galactic spiral arms requires no dark matter at all; it is merely the shape residue left by a grand "drag of inner high-speed celestial bodies on outer low-speed ones" during the expansion of the envelope controlled by the galaxy's root domain.

1. 旋臂的本质：不是波，而是“强行收编留下的刹车痕”

1. The Essence of Spiral Arms: Not Waves, But "Brake Marks Left by Forced Incorporation"

在宇宙早期，本宇宙还在超级大宇宙的内圈圈层，星系并不像今天这样是一个致密的刚性圆盘的平坦化旋转，因为超级大宇宙中心附近超级中心的根域能量密度是很强的，星系核的根域受到极大的压制，并不能延展多远，这直接导致星系旋转无法平坦化，跟太阳系的行星的旋转一样遵守着开普勒定律。 星系的核心区（黑洞及其周边）转得快，而广阔的星系外围则慢，遵循着内快外慢的“开普勒定律”。

In the early universe, when our local universe was still in the inner layers of the super-universe, galaxies were not the flat, rigid-disk rotation they are today. Because the root-domain energy density near the super-center of the super-universe was very strong, the root domain of the galactic nucleus was heavily suppressed and could not extend far. This directly prevented the galaxy's rotation from becoming flat; it followed Kepler's laws just like the planets in the solar system. The core region (the black hole and its surroundings) rotated fast, while the vast periphery rotated slowly — obeying the "inner-fast, outer-slow" Keplerian law.

随着本宇宙被不断向外排斥，超级宇宙超级中心的基底能量密度不断下降，也即深空能量密度的不断下降，而星系的自身根域能量密度由星系自身中心黑洞决定，基本稳定，何况黑洞还会不断吸积，导致星系的根域统治范围越来越大。星系核心的“宏观根域斥力包络面”开始向外扩张。这个包络面内天体运转平坦化，v与r成正比，向外扩张，俘获开普勒定律正比1/√r 外围天体，形成“拖拽”后的形变。旋转的星系盘上会拖拽出了几道宏大、明亮、向后弯曲的裂谷——这就是我们在望远镜里看到的“卍”字形旋臂！

As our local universe was continuously repelled outward, the background energy density of the super-universe's super-center kept decreasing — i.e., the energy density of deep space decreased. Meanwhile, the galaxy's own root-domain energy density, determined by its central black hole, remained roughly stable, especially since the black hole continues to accrete, causing the galaxy's root-domain dominion to expand. The "macroscopic root-domain repulsive envelope" of the galactic core began to expand outward. Within this envelope, celestial motion became flat (v proportional to r). As the envelope expanded outward, it captured peripheral objects following Kepler's 1/√r law, creating a "drag-induced" deformation. On the rotating galactic disk, several grand, bright, backward-curving rifts were dragged out — these are the "swastika-shaped" spiral arms we see through telescopes!

也就是说，旋臂根本不是什么神秘的波，它就是高速平坦化旋转刚体星系盘强行俘获低速大天体时，留在宇宙深空的**“物理刹车痕”**。

In other words, spiral arms are not some mysterious waves; they are simply "physical brake marks" left in deep space when a rapidly spinning, flat rigid galactic disk forcibly captures slow-moving massive objects.

2. 星系平坦化：角动量守恒的妥协

2. Galactic Flattening: A Compromise of Angular Momentum Conservation

在这个“强行收编”的过程中，星系核心吃进了大量外围的低速质量。为了维持整个星系系统的“总角动量守恒”，那个原本，必须牺牲自己转速，让星系整体的转速“变慢一点点”。

During this process of "forced incorporation," the galactic core devours large amounts of low-speed mass from the periphery. To conserve the total angular momentum of the entire galactic system, the core must sacrifice some of its own rotational speed, slowing down the overall rotation "just a little."

天文学家观测到星系外围转速没有下降（平坦化旋转），惊呼“一定有暗物质在牵引”——其实，这不过是星系在吞噬低速天体后，达成的引力-斥力提供向心力，导致v与r成正比罢了。科学家至今对万有斥力才是全宇宙普遍存在的基础力一无所知。它在星系层面，对星系是向心力=万有引力-万有斥力的高斯积分方程，让星系平坦化旋转（本人之前理论及天文数据python代码对175星系的大数据统计均支持该结论）。

Astronomers observe that the peripheral rotational speed does not decline (flat rotation curve) and exclaim, "There must be dark matter pulling!" — In reality, this is merely the result of gravity-repulsion providing centripetal force after the galaxy has swallowed low-speed objects, leading to v proportional to r. Scientists remain utterly ignorant that universal repulsion is a fundamental force pervasive throughout the cosmos. At the galactic level, the centripetal force equals gravitational force minus repulsive force integrated via Gauss's equation, which produces flat rotation (my previous theoretical work and Python analysis of 175 galaxies' observational data strongly support this conclusion).

3. 终极谜团：为什么旋臂总是极其对称的？

3. The Ultimate Mystery: Why Are Spiral Arms Always So Symmetrical?

这是传统天文学最头疼的问题：即使形成了旋臂，宇宙充满了随机性，凭什么银河系的旋臂能长得那么均匀、对称？

This is the most vexing problem for traditional astronomy: Even if spiral arms form, the universe is full of randomness. Why can the Milky Way's arms grow so evenly and symmetrically?

答案隐藏在一个极其简单的力学常识里：宏观质心动态自平衡（宇宙跷跷板）。

The answer lies in an extremely simple mechanical common sense: Macroscopic dynamic self-balancing of the center of mass (cosmic seesaw).

真实的外围深空是不均匀的。假设星系在向外收编时，左侧刚好有一坨超大质量的星际物质。此时，整个星系系统的“旋转质心”，必然会偏离那个处于几何中心的超级大黑洞，向着左侧那条肥大的旋臂发生偏移。

Real deep-space peripheries are inhomogeneous. Suppose that while the galaxy is incorporating outward material, there happens to be a huge clump of interstellar mass on the left side. Then the "rotational center of mass" of the entire galactic system will inevitably shift away from the supermassive black hole at the geometric center, moving toward the left, more massive arm.

这就导致了一个极其精妙的错位： 既然星系绕着新的偏心质心旋转，那么几何中心的超级黑洞，在空间距离上，就被迫“靠向”了右侧那条弱小的旋臂！这意味着右侧弱小的旋臂获得了中心黑洞根域无时延能量辐照范围的支援，有能力辐照到更远，抓到更远的天体成为旋臂组成部分，优先抓到飘过来的天体，这一优势加强了弱小旋臂的捕捉优势。

This leads to an extremely delicate misalignment: Since the galaxy now rotates around the new eccentric center of mass, the geometrically centered supermassive black hole is forced, in spatial distance, to "lean toward" the right, weaker arm! This means the weak right arm gains support from the central black hole's root-domain energy irradiation (which operates without time delay), enabling it to irradiate farther, capture more distant objects to become part of the arm, and preferentially grab drifting objects. This advantage enhances the capturing capability of the weaker arm.

星系中心黑洞，那可是星系引力和斥力的超级源头，星系的绝对控制力。当它离弱小旋臂一侧更近，外围的散落天体容易吸聚到这条弱小的旋臂上。于是，弱小侧因为得到了星系核心黑洞的“近距离扶持”，生长速度变快！

The central black hole is the supreme source of both gravity and repulsion in the galaxy — the absolute controller of the galaxy. When it is closer to the weaker arm, scattered peripheral objects are more easily accreted onto that weak arm. Thus, the weak side, receiving "close-range support" from the galactic core black hole, grows faster!

结语：大道至简的宇宙哲学

Conclusion: The Profound Simplicity of Cosmic Philosophy

星系不需要暗物质这种缝合怪来维持形状。它有着自己极其完美的负反馈系统：

Galaxies do not need the Frankenstein monster called dark matter to maintain their shapes. They possess their own perfectly elegant negative feedback system:

  • 一侧旋臂质量过大不对称 → 导致旋转中心偏移向大旋臂 → 核心黑洞靠近弱侧旋臂 → 弱侧旋臂获得了星系黑洞引力/斥力的支援增强了吸聚 → 恢复宏观对称。
  • One side's arm becomes too massive (asymmetry) → Rotational center shifts toward the heavy arm → Core black hole moves closer to the weak arm → Weak arm receives enhanced gravitational/repulsive support from the black hole, increasing accretion → Macroscopic symmetry is restored.

从极度混沌的宇宙散沙，到今天我们看到的极其庄严、完美的旋涡星系，宇宙仅仅用了一套最简单的“俘获拖拽”卍字型的形成，与“偏心跷跷板”机制导致旋臂的对称，就完成了大自然的鬼斧神工。

From the utterly chaotic cosmic dust to the majestic, perfect spiral galaxies we see today, the universe has accomplished nature's masterpiece using only the simplest mechanism: the formation of swastika-shaped arms through "capture and drag," and the "eccentric seesaw" that ensures arm symmetry.
