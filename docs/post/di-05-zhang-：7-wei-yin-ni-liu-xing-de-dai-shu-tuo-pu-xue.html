$$(\delta_k \omega)(\sigma) = \sum_{i=0}^{k+1} (-1)^i \omega([P_0, \dots, \hat{P}_i, \dots, P_{k+1}])$$

$$ 0 \to \Omega^0(\mathcal{M}_7) \xrightarrow{\delta_0} \Omega^1(\mathcal{M}_7) \xrightarrow{\delta_1} \dots \xrightarrow{\delta_6} \Omega^7(\mathcal{M}_7) \to 0 $$

（由于 $\mathcal{M}_7$ 代数维数为 7，复形在 $k=7$ 处自然截断。）
