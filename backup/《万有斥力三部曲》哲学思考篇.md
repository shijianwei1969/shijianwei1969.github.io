$\begin{cases} \mathbf{a}_1 = \mathbf{a}_{1,\text{引力}} + \mathbf{a}_{\text{根域背景}} \\ \mathbf{a}_2 = \mathbf{a}_{2,\text{引力}} + \mathbf{a}_{\text{根域背景}} \end{cases}  \implies \Delta \mathbf{a}_{12} = \mathbf{a}_1 - \mathbf{a}_2 = \Delta \mathbf{a}_{\text{引力}}$

$$\text{电磁向内收敛的力} = \text{斥力向外托举的力}$$
